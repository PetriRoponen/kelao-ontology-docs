Abstract

The Finnish Social Security Core Ontology (kelao) describes the core concepts of the Finnish social security system and the relationships between them in a machine-interpretable form: benefits and their components, eligibility criteria, the application and decision process, payments and recovery, appeals, customer roles, services and service channels, the administrative area hierarchy, and the underlying legislation. The ontology complements Kela's terminological vocabulary: the vocabulary defines concepts for human readers, while the ontology makes the relationships, constraints and inference rules between concepts machine-checkable and queryable.
